# RRaw Tether

Tethered shooting for Mac, Capture One style: plug in your camera and every shot lands in your session the moment you take it. Sessions with Capture / Selects / Output / Trash, automatic backup, shot list, colour tags, histogram, vectorscope, false colour, crop guides and a see-through reference overlay. Works with Sony (PC Remote) and Canon EOS, plus other cameras through macOS.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the newest version](https://github.com/robertarnar98/RRawTether-Download/releases/latest)**: click **RRaw-Tether.dmg** under *Assets*.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click **RRaw-Tether.dmg**.
2. Drag **RRaw Tether** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. That's it: RRaw Tether is signed and approved by Apple, so it opens with no security warning.

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a card pops up in the middle of RRaw Tether. Click **Update Now** and it updates itself in a few seconds and reopens. It never updates while a camera is plugged in or shots are still saving. You can also choose **RRaw Tether ▸ Check for Updates…** any time.

Needs macOS 13 Ventura or newer · Apple Silicon and Intel Macs · by Tungl Media
